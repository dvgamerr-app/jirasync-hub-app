Your Jira tickets, on your desktop, even when Jira isn’t.

Pull the tickets assigned to you, edit and log work offline, then push back only the fields you actually changed.

Edit freely. Push precisely.

The app tracks changes per field. Logging an hour on a ticket sends the worklog and nothing else, so a stale local copy never overwrites someone’s priority or story points.

What you get

Everything is stored locally first, and your Jira token lives in the OS credential store.

Incremental syncHourly background pull of only what changed, plus a full sync on demand.
Offline editingStatus, type, severity, story level, mandays, notes and worklogs.
Selective pushPer task or all at once, with field-level change tracking.
Honest time totalsTeammates’ worklogs are read-only and never counted in your totals.
CSV exportMonthly worklog export that opens cleanly in Excel, with a warning for unpushed time.
Multi-accountSeveral Jira Cloud sites, with story point fields mapped per project.

Task detail and worklogs

Open a ticket, log time with a live preview of how your input is read, and export the month as CSV when it’s time to report.

Get started in four steps

You need a Jira Cloud site (*.atlassian.net), your Atlassian email and an API token.

  1. Download the installer for your OS from Releases.
  2. Open Settings → Add Account, enter instance, email and token, then Save & Connect.
  3. Press Sync to pull your projects, tasks and worklogs.
  4. Edit tasks, then press the upload button to push only what changed.
